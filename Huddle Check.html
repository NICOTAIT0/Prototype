Huddle Check

Paste what your team produced: a meeting transcript, your charter, and any Breakout Learning or assessment feedback. Huddle Check shows who is speaking and who is not, where the process is leaking, and what to try in the next meeting.

How to use this tool (about 3 minutes)

Steps

  1. Record a team meeting and copy the transcript from Zoom, Teams, Otter or your phone's transcription.
  2. Put one speaker per line as Name: what they said. Use first names or initials.
  3. Paste your team charter and any feedback (Breakout Learning comments, assessment takeaways).
  4. Press Run instant check. It runs in your browser in a second.
  5. Press Get AI coaching for a written read tied to the class frameworks.

What each input unlocks

  • Transcript: airtime, quiet members, who closes decisions, concerns that got brushed aside.
  • Charter: which parts of a team contract are missing.
  • Feedback: how members experienced the meeting, which the transcript cannot show. Used by AI coaching only.
  • Roster: finds people who never spoke. Match the names used in the transcript.

Good to know

  • The instant check uses simple pattern matching, so treat it as a prompt for discussion, not a verdict.
  • Nothing is stored. The instant check never leaves your browser.
  • AI coaching sends your text to Claude through your own account. Remove names you would not share.
  • No AI access? Copy the prompt at the bottom into ChatGPT or any AI tool.
